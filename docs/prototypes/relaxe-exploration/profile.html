Identity without
crowding the feed.

The header expands at the top and collapses while reading, matching your selected direction. Posts and Comments remain the two native content tabs. Their controls scroll away with the header.

Header actions selected

Selected: Follow sits beside the username; Save user is a separate bookmark action. Your own profile shows Edit profile instead. Karma, cake day and the bio remain below the name.

Sort and More stay in the compact toolbar. More also switches Posts/Comments while scrolled. Bottom Search opens over this profile, scoped to its user.

Comment context selected

Each comment shows its subreddit and parent post title before the comment text, with points and a conversation destination below. Post context first is selected. Final density and typography remain Android review work.
